# Nocative — Product Hunt Launch

## Tagline
**The fiction platform where writers keep 90-95% of every sale.**

## Description (short — PH character limit)
Nocative is a publishing platform for fiction writers. Writers publish chapters, set their own prices, and sell directly to readers via Stripe Connect — no middleman, no subscriptions. Readers get free previews, then rent for a year or buy permanent access. Writers keep 95% of rentals and 90% of permanent sales.

## Full Description

### The Problem
Wattpad doesn't pay writers. Amazon KDP takes 30-65% and buries indie authors. Substack is for essays, not fiction. There's no clean, simple platform where a fiction writer can publish chapter-by-chapter and actually earn from day one.

### What Nocative Does
- **Writers publish chapters** into collections (stories), organize with genres and labels
- **Set your own pricing** — minimum $14/year rental, $21 permanent access
- **Readers sample free chapters** before committing
- **Rent or buy** — 1-year rental includes all current + future chapters published during the rental window. Permanent access = forever, including all future chapters.
- **Stripe Connect payouts** — writer's share auto-transfers to their bank account. No manual payout requests.
- **95% of rentals, 90% of permanent sales** go directly to the writer

### Key Features
- Rich text editor with markdown support
- Genre & label system for discoverability
- "Mark as Sellable" — one click to make a collection available for purchase
- Free chapters required (3 free minimum before any premium) so readers can sample
- Published content is permanent — protects both readers and writers
- Collections with active purchases cannot be deleted
- Rental → Permanent upgrade path for readers

- **Tech:** Cloudflare Workers + D1 + React SPA, Stripe Connect
